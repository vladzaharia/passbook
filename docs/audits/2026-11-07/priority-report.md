# Global P0–P3 audit priorities and execution order

## Decision and severity

**P0: none established.** No reviewed evidence demonstrated an unauthenticated, uncontrolled catastrophic compromise that meets an immediate P0 threshold. Multiple P1s remain and the checkout is not security-approved for broader partner, scanner or wallet-provider exposure. The primary source report locally reproduced stale-recipient delivery disclosure, scan DTO/photo leakage, monitor promotion and cross-org holder portfolio behavior; see [verification](verification.md) and [index](README.md).

- **P0 — immediate critical incident:** confirmed broad, unauthenticated or readily weaponized compromise with catastrophic cross-tenant impact. None established; this is not a safety approval.
- **P1 — release/safety blocker:** confirmed or strongly source-supported unauthorized data/credential/admission, or a required safety invariant that must be met before the affected flow or new offering is enabled. Keep condition and test status explicit.
- **P2 — high-priority correctness, recovery, tenancy or target-feature foundation:** material risk or required target capability; complete before relying on or advertising the relevant service.
- **P3 — lower-risk usability, commercial, governance and polish:** address before broader scale/contract promises; preserve owner/legal decisions separately.

**Scope labels:** `Current defect` describes behavior in the audited source; `Target release gate` is missing functionality required by the user’s stated future product, not a claim of current exploitation. `[INFERENCE]` means a source-grounded interleaving or operational effect not exercised. Production account/tenant/IdP/provider state is unverified.

The dedicated control/settings UX review is complete and linked under P3. It adds no new severity count; its target mockups are design evidence, not product implementation or release verification.

## P1 — contain unsafe authority, privacy and admission paths first

### 1. Block stale or cross-person pass access

**Current defects:** A queued email retains its original recipient but delivery/retry reloads the current download URL and artifact. This was reproduced in an isolated local mailer. Holder reassignment does not retire Apple fetch credentials, serial, registration or scan code; a former holder can retain a usable pass and receive future personalized updates. Rotating only the public link does not fence installed copies. These are separate channels and must be fixed together.

**Required result:** Bind every send, attachment and provider link to owner/recipient, artifact and credential generation. A transfer cancels stale delivery and retires prior credentials online before issuing B fresh credentials. Do not claim physical removal from an offline Wallet. Test resend, bundle, Apple fetch, scanner credential and future provider links after transfer/update.

Evidence: [local reproduction](verification.md#security-reproductions), [pass-delivery review](reviews/10-red-pass-delivery.md), [revocation/credential review](reviews/wallet-revocation-credentials.md), [authoring trust](reviews/authoring-pass-trust.md).

### 2. Enforce test/live isolation and explicit issuance review

**Current defects:** A test-mode pass/key can be linked to a same-org live connector and consume live source fields through refresh/scheduled paths. A Test draft can become Live if another tab changes mode because issuance intent is not bound to the reviewed mode. Reviewed template version can also differ from version resolved at submission; some upload/manifest replacement routes can activate a version without the designer’s separate release step.

**Required result:** Enforce org **and mode** on create, bind, selectors, refresh, poll, webhook, scheduled sync, response projection and mutation, including existing bindings. Bind issuance to an explicit reviewed mode and immutable template version; mode/version changes invalidate review. Normalize every authoring route to draft → review exact revision → explicit authorized activation. Acceptance must prove no Live artifact or message from a Test review and no Live data in Test outputs.

Evidence: [red-team delivery](reviews/10-red-pass-delivery.md), [blue-team](reviews/11-blue-platform.md), [tenant resource isolation](reviews/multitenancy-resource-isolation.md), [authoring trust](reviews/authoring-pass-trust.md), [template collaboration](reviews/authoring-template-collaboration.md).

### 3. Make all scanner responses obey one audience/role policy

**Current defects:** Local reproduction found filtered display values still present in `passView.passJson`; a role-gated member photo was fetchable through `thumbnail@3x.png`. Source review also found unfiltered action values/flags, derived-title lineage and stored lookup fields. A name-only admin PATCH promoted a read-only monitor and committed a check-in in a local harness. Capacity-full overrides inherit ordinary re-entry authorization; native input/verdict acknowledgements and uncertain retries can lose correlation/scope.

**Required result:** Authorize every DTO field, preview, asset alias and live projection server-side using recipient role, source lineage and mode; remove raw pass JSON unless a safe role-projected preview is explicitly needed. Preserve immutable monitor purpose and PATCH intent. Require independent manager authority for capacity exceptions; bind native acknowledgements and retries to the original input/event/config generation. Retest every affected scan/action/undo/photo/history/operator/customer route, not only the rendered panel.

Evidence: [local verification](verification.md#scanner-secretrole-filtering-and-photo-alias), [scanner security](reviews/12-blue-scanner-native.md), [buddy/action correlation](reviews/06-cohesion-buddy.md), [native code](reviews/08-code-web-native.md), [scan-display security](reviews/authoring-scan-display.md), [surface model](reviews/authoring-surface-preview-model.md).

### 4. Make mutable ownership, binding, version and lifecycle part of commit authority

**Current defects:** Warm Worker isolate can authorize a template using stale cached `orgId` after reassignment; cross-isolate exploit was not run. Publication checks before async work are not always repeated in the committing CAS. Source-grounded interleavings can publish after deletion/unbind, and a delayed older template reissue can downgrade a newer revision. OIDC email can be linked before mailbox proof; exploitability depends on deployed IdP claims, which were not checked. A check-in can be evaluated before void and commit afterward `[INFERENCE; not executed]`.

**Required result:** Keep mutable permission/ownership metadata out of cross-request caches or recheck authoritative state at final write; pin and commit against org/mode, active lifecycle, owner/binding, source/template/provider generation and expected revision. Atomically revalidate pass validity/deletion and active credential generation with the admission gate. Require verified identity proof before ownership linking. Test deterministic interleavings, cache reassignment across isolates and IdP claim cases against the deployed identity policy.

Evidence: [backend](reviews/07-code-backend.md), [tenant resource isolation](reviews/multitenancy-resource-isolation.md), [tenant security](reviews/09-red-auth-tenant.md), [template lifecycle](reviews/authoring-template-collaboration.md), [credential lifecycle](reviews/wallet-revocation-credentials.md).

### 5. Fail closed on required credential protections

**Current defect:** Required Apple NFC authentication without `sharingProhibited` emits a warning, so issuance can proceed without the required older-OS share protection. Conditional NFC personalization can also be preempted by a predictable caller-selected serial `[P2 in source review; feature must remain gated]`.

**Required result:** Make NFC-auth/share constraints blocking in import, preview, build and issuance. Use server-generated unpredictable credentials and prove entitlement/device behavior. Do not enable NFC/VAS/Smart Tap until each channel’s identity, anti-replay, sharing, revocation and verifier requirements are enforced; no Cloudflare perimeter rule substitutes for that.

Evidence: [wallet feasibility](reviews/28-wallet-feature-research.md), [wallet degradation](reviews/wallet-security-degradation.md), [credential lifecycle](reviews/wallet-revocation-credentials.md), [conditional personalization](reviews/10-red-pass-delivery.md).

### 6. Partner-org isolation is a launch gate for delegated tenant administration

The current global-admin model and platform-wide null-org keys/shared identities are explicit v1 design choices; reviewed org-bound key paths did not establish a general foreign-org bypass. They do not satisfy the new requested strict-organization-management target. Do not grant partners global-admin access or treat `orgId` UI selection as a permission boundary.

**Target gate:** Before enabling tenant-admin users or self-service org operations, establish an authoritative membership roster and distinct platform-admin principal. Enforce action + organization + resource + active membership on every API, repository, asset, export, background job, template/library operation and provider account grant. Explicitly model platform-shared resources. Preserve cross-org customer portfolio access only through that verified customer’s own `owner_user_id`; never promote it to organization authority. Backfill must not infer that every current platform admin belongs to every org.

Evidence: [RBAC](reviews/multitenancy-rbac-security.md), [control plane](reviews/multitenancy-control-plane.md), [schema](reviews/multitenancy-schema-relationships.md), [resource isolation](reviews/multitenancy-resource-isolation.md), [customer portfolio](reviews/multitenancy-customer-portfolio.md).

## P2 — repair durable correctness and complete required foundations

1. **Cross-tenant personal data and lifecycle gates:** Remove global primary email/actor enrichment from tenant-scoped pass DTOs; scope shared-template usage/version counts or omit them for tenant keys. Gate holder Add-all/bundle per pass on active organization without breaking the holder’s intentional A+B portfolio. Preserve tenant and mode in every query, cache and export. Source-confirmed; the exact intended visibility of shared-template aggregates is an owner policy decision.
2. **Durable integration intake and honest outcome:** Persist webhook payload, dedupe record and dispatch chunks atomically; redispatch incomplete work. Do not acknowledge or move cursor past deferred/unapplied records. Handle missing records without starving later ones. Retain terminal outcomes after Queue DLQ drain; make `202`, pushed/sent and applied distinct. Add connection-wide quota coordination only if partner limits require it.
3. **Artifact and release consistency:** Reconcile ambiguous D1 create responses before deleting R2 bytes. Garbage-collect only provably unreferenced objects after a grace window. Persist the full bulk/reissue cursor before success; fence target template/provider revision and generation in every resumed operation. Make D1/R2/credential backups and restore a tested coordinated procedure.
4. **Quotas, retries and observability:** Separate budget deferral from failed attempts; schedule a durable due operation before acknowledging wake-up. Fix API idempotency reservation recovery, rate-limit/KEK enumeration, per-hook health, actual scan analytics and safe-fetch total deadline/DNS destination enforcement. Define logs/alerts/retention without capturing capability links or personal payloads. Cloudflare service fit and caps are in [Cloudflare follow-up](reviews/cloudflare-service-fit-followup.md); these services aid but do not solve app logic.
5. **Wallet-provider architecture is a target-release gate for non-Apple claims.** The current product is Apple-only. Introduce canonical semantic entitlement/revisions, provider/account mappings and many provider instances; keep Apple full-fidelity and migrate its identifiers, signatures, registrations and update cursor intact. Google/Samsung must be real native 1.5-class adapters, not `.pkpass` exports. Block required unsupported semantics; disclose optional omissions; isolate per-provider update/revoke failures. All provider scan credentials map to the same canonical pass and atomic admission gate. Represent install state as provider/account/device observations or `unknown`, not one `inWallet` flag.
6. **Customer wallet choice and delivery:** On Apple recommend Apple; on eligible Samsung devices recommend Samsung; on eligible generic Android recommend Google. Keep every other eligible provider reachable in an accessible “Other wallets” accordion on portal and scriptless public pages. UA only ranks choices; it does not prove install/eligibility. Email offers every eligible provider using official brand assets and each provider’s real email-safe flow, exact account/device/market requirements and plain-text counterpart. Current Apple badge is interim and license/localization/contrast need correction. Product readiness is not satisfied by button artwork alone.
7. **Nontechnical template and display authoring:** Build one neutral semantic authoring model with provider-native front/details previews and distinct staff scan, operator lookup/activity and customer live projections. The current WYSIWYG designer is Apple `pass.json`; Scan-screen edit does not open a working display editor; customer display schema fields are not a runtime projection. Use server-side role/source-lineage previews, safe synthetic fixtures, immutable draft/review/approval/version/rollback and non-technical controls. Verdict, authority, grants and sensitive projection rules remain system-owned.
8. **Authoring concurrency and review:** Durable shared drafts, author/reviewer/publisher permissions, review tied to immutable revision, expected-active-version CAS and release-generation fencing are required before collaborative tenant authoring. Prevent import/manifest routes from silently activating/reissuing; make version diff, provenance, in-flight rollout and rollback truthful.
9. **New-user and account recovery UX:** Show actual effective defaults and mode/version in final issuance review; keep custom images/front/back/expiry visible, block malformed composite fields and focus errors. Keep Add-again and replacement-device actions available independent of other installs. Stabilize Add-all membership so installing one chunk does not skip the rest. Distinguish portal removal, provider removal, credential retirement and entitlement cancellation.
10. **Cloudflare configuration/recovery controls:** Resolve deployment pre-hook argument loss and required-secret shutdown docs; validate required secrets before migration. Status must retain terminal delivery failures after DLQ consumption and prove KEK readiness without values. Verify real WAF/Access/Turnstile, account entitlements, jurisdiction, backup, email quota and vendor delivery with owner evidence; account settings are currently unverified.

## P3 — close before broader commercial promises

- Publish a managed-service boundary or build the required delegation; establish partner/agency contracting authority, technical owner/deputy, support escalation, response objectives, rollout approvals, pricing/billing/trial, compatibility/deprecation, export/retention/deletion, and evidence-retention terms. The repository does not establish private off-platform agreements; obtain owner/legal evidence before calling these contractual defects. See [contract operations](reviews/business-contract-operations.md) and [commercial readiness](reviews/business-commercial-readiness.md).
- Complete the visual/accessibility follow-up across public, portal, admin, scanner and email, with keyboard/screen-reader behavior, mobile states, scanner focus/speech and correct Buddy usage. Keep these behind safety/authority gates; do not substitute polish for safe scan display or customer-visible provider eligibility. The dedicated [control UX redesign](reviews/control-ux-redesign.md) maps current admin routes and non-admin capabilities and records three screenshot-based critique passes; its proposed frames are not runtime evidence.
- Add owner-visible provider approval/readiness, license and market requirements, verified external delivery and physical iPhone/Android/Galaxy/watch/scanner acceptance. Do not claim these tests were run here.
