{{mark}}PassBook07 · Motion

Every movement has a job.

Show a response, an arrival or a real change. Then settle.

Chrome

80–280ms

Controls answer quickly.

Art entrance

400–600ms

Objects reveal what they are.

Signature

480–900ms

Passes deal, flip and mint.

1Anticipate

The buddy looks toward the task.

2Act

A pass points, reaches or waves.

3React

The expression follows the result.

4Settle

The scene returns to rest.

One signature moment per screen

Buddy scenes last 1–4 seconds after landing and settle within five seconds of mount. Only pending-state indicators loop. Success follows confirmed data; input never waits.

Reduced motion keeps the meaning

Use the resting pose and fades of 150 ms or less. Remove decorative travel, tilt, wink and mint sweeps. Keep essential pending indicators. Email is always static.

Pass deal 480 ms · Flip 640 ms · Mint 900 ms · Apple Wallet badge stays still08 / 11

{{mark}}PassBook09 · Email and organization identity

Let the sender of the pass lead.

PassBook welcomes the person. The organization owns the pass and its delivery.

Organization first

Public pass pages, pass emails and installed passes lead with the organization's identity.

Keep pass art and colors true. The page theme must not recolor the pass.

Badge handoff

Use the existing Apple Wallet artwork unchanged. The repo marks it as interim: the owner still needs to supply Apple's official kit and complete the license record.
